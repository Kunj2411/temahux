## Content Publishing Guidelines

1. Only publish articles that reflect genuine TEMAHUX expertise
2. Every article must provide real value — not thin promotional content
3. Use factual claims only — no invented statistics, awards, or client results
4. Each article should link to at least 2 relevant internal pages
5. Each article needs a unique title, meta description, and canonical URL
6. Add `Article` or `BlogPosting` JSON-LD schema to each published article
7. Update the sitemap after publishing new content
